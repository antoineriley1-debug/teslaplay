TeslaPlay Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, TeslaPlay receives your basic Google profile information (name, email address, and profile picture) and read-only access to your YouTube account through Google OAuth.

How it is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only in your own browser. TeslaPlay has no server and no database; no account data is sent to or kept by the developer.

How it is used

This information is used only to run the app for you: showing who is signed in, searching YouTube, and opening your playlists.

Sharing

Your information is not sold or shared with anyone. TeslaPlay shows no ads of its own and uses no analytics or tracking.

Google API Services

TeslaPlay's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can remove TeslaPlay's access at any time with Sign out in the app, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
